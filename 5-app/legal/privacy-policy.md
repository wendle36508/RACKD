## The short version

We know privacy policies are long, so here is the plain-English summary. The full policy below is what counts.

- **What RACKD does.** RACKD shows you secondhand fashion listings from resale sites (currently Vinted, Depop and eBay). You swipe right on what you like and left on what you don't, and RACKD learns your style so your feed gets better.
- **What we collect.** Your account details (like your email), your swipes and the style profile we build from them, basic device and usage information, and your subscription status. RACKD is a paid subscription: you start it at sign-up through our payment processor, Stripe, which handles your card details are handled by our payment processor, We never see or store your full card number.
- **What we don't collect.** We don't ask for your contacts, your precise location, your photos or your camera. We don't buy data about you from data brokers.
- **How we use it.** To run your account, personalize your feed, find new listings that match your style, bill your subscription, keep the Service secure, and improve RACKD.
- **We don't sell your personal information,** and we don't use it for targeted advertising.
- **Buying happens on the seller's site.** When you tap through to buy, you leave RACKD and go to Vinted, Depop, eBay or another platform. Their privacy policy applies there, not ours. Some of our links are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission.
- **Your control.** You can see, download, correct or delete your data, reset your style profile, and cancel your subscription at any time. Email **[PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]** or use the in-app settings.
- **Age.** You must be at least 13 (or older where local law requires) to use RACKD.

## 5. Your style profile, personalization and automated decisions

Personalization is the core of RACKD, so we want to be clear about how it works.

- **How the profile is built.** We look at the listings you like and pass on, and at any preferences you give us. An image-recognition model (FashionCLIP) turns each **listing photo** into a "style fingerprint". By comparing the fingerprints of what you like and pass on, we estimate which styles, colors and items you're likely to want. Until you've liked a few items (currently three), you see general "starter" listings.
- **What it's used for.** Ranking your feed, building the searches that find new listings for you, and deciding whether a listing someone else found is a good match for you.
- **What it is not used for.** We do not use your style profile to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you (such as decisions about credit, employment, housing or insurance). We do not use it to infer sensitive characteristics, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising outside RACKD.
- **Your control.** You can reset your style profile at any time (in the app, "Start over"), which clears your likes and passes so RACKD starts learning again. You can also ask us to delete your profile entirely (see section 12). Under EEA and UK law you have the right to object to profiling based on our legitimate interests.

## 10. How we protect your information

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information, including:

- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and, through our hosting and database providers, encryption at rest.
- Passwords stored only in hashed form; we never see your password.
- Secret keys kept outside our code and limited to the people and systems that need them.
- Access to personal information limited to people who need it to run RACKD.
- Payments handled by Stripe, which is certified to PCI DSS Level 1, so card data never touches our servers.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a security breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.
